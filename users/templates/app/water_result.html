🚫 NOT SAFE DRINKING WATER

ALERT

Prevention

Clean and safe water is crucial for health. Prevention involves:
  • Boiling water before drinking
  • Using water filters or purifiers
  • Avoiding untreated water sources

Precautions

  • Always wash hands with clean water and soap
  • Avoid swimming in untreated lakes or rivers
  • Store drinking water in clean, covered containers

Causes

Unsafe water can be contaminated by:
  • Sewage or industrial waste
  • Agricultural runoff with pesticides
  • Bacterial or viral infections

Effects

Drinking unsafe water can lead to:
  • Gastrointestinal infections (e.g., diarrhea)
  • Waterborne diseases (e.g., cholera, typhoid)
  • Long-term health issues (e.g., kidney damage)

Other Information

  • Ensure proper sanitation and waste disposal
  • Support community efforts for clean water access
  • Stay informed about local water quality reports
