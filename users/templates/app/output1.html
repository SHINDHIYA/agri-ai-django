{% if prediction_text == 'Apple' %}

Apple

Malus domestica

Cultivation Details

Soil Type: Well-drained loamy soil
Climate: Temperate (cold winters, moderate summers)
Season: Plant in early spring
Full Sunlight pH 6.0-7.0 Prune annually

Care Instructions

Watering: Moderate (1-2 inches weekly)
Fertilizer: 10-10-10 NPK in spring
Spacing: 15-20 feet between trees

Common Pests

Aphids Codling Moth Apple Scab

Detailed Information

Apple trees require well-drained soil and full sunlight for optimal growth. They need winter chilling (below 7°C) for proper fruiting. Regular pruning helps maintain tree shape and improves air circulation. Apples are susceptible to several pests and diseases, so integrated pest management is recommended. Harvest typically occurs 100-200 days after flowering depending on variety.

{% elif prediction_text == 'Banana' %}

Banana

Musa spp.

Cultivation Details

Soil Type: Rich, well-drained loam
Climate: Tropical (25-30°C ideal)
Season: Year-round in tropics
Full Sun pH 5.5-7.0 High Humidity

Care Instructions

Watering: Regular, keep moist
Fertilizer: High potassium (3-1-6 ratio)
Spacing: 8-10 feet between plants

Common Pests

Panama Disease Black Sigatoka Nematodes

Detailed Information

Bananas grow best in tropical climates with high humidity and temperatures between 26-30°C. They require protection from strong winds which can damage leaves. Plants typically fruit 9-12 months after planting. After fruiting, the main stem dies and is replaced by new suckers. Regular mulching helps retain soil moisture and suppress weeds.

Black Gram

Vigna mungo

Cultivation Details

Soil Type: Well-drained loamy soil
Climate: Warm (25-35°C ideal)
Season: Kharif and summer season
Full Sun pH 6.5-7.5 Drought Tolerant

Care Instructions

Watering: Moderate, avoid waterlogging
Fertilizer: 20-40 kg N, 40-60 kg P₂O₅/ha
Spacing: 30 cm between rows, 10 cm between plants

Common Pests

Yellow Mosaic Virus Pod Borer Aphids

Detailed Information

Black gram is an important pulse crop grown mainly in India. It's a short duration crop (80-120 days) that improves soil fertility through nitrogen fixation. The crop is relatively drought resistant but sensitive to waterlogging. It's often grown as an intercrop with cereals. Proper weed management is crucial during the first 30 days.

{% elif prediction_text == 'Chickpea' %}

Chickpea

Cicer arietinum

Cultivation Details

Soil Type: Well-drained sandy loam
Climate: Cool season crop (18-25°C)
Season: Rabi season (winter)
Full Sun pH 6.0-7.0 Frost Sensitive

Care Instructions

Watering: Light, avoid waterlogging
Fertilizer: 20-25 kg N, 40-50 kg P₂O₅/ha
Spacing: 30-45 cm between rows

Common Pests

Pod Borer Ascochyta Blight Fusarium Wilt

Detailed Information

Chickpea is a cool season legume crop that matures in 90-120 days. It's highly valued for its protein-rich seeds. The crop is sensitive to waterlogging and heavy rainfall during flowering. Coconut

Cocos nucifera

Cultivation Details

Soil Type: Sandy loam, well-drained
Climate: Tropical (27±5°C ideal)
Season: Year-round in tropics
Full Sun pH 5.0-8.0 High Humidity

Care Instructions

Watering: Regular, 100-150L/palm/week
Fertilizer: 1.3kg NPK 12-12-17+2MgO/year
Spacing: 7.5-9m between palms

Common Pests

Rhinoceros Beetle Red Palm Weevil Bud Rot

Detailed Information

Coconut palms are perennial crops that begin bearing fruits in 5-7 years and continue for 60-70 years. They require abundant sunlight (2000+ hrs/year) and evenly distributed rainfall (1500-2500mm). Palms are sensitive to waterlogging and need good drainage. Regular intercropping during early years improves soil fertility and provides additional income.

{% elif prediction_text == 'Coffee' %}

Coffee

Coffea arabica/robusta

Cultivation Details

Soil Type: Volcanic, well-drained
Climate: 15-24°C (Arabica), 24-30°C (Robusta)
Season: Plant in rainy season
Partial Shade pH 6.0-6.5 Frost Sensitive

Care Instructions

Watering: Regular, 1500-2500mm/year
Fertilizer: NPK 15-15-15, micronutrients
Spacing: 2-3m between plants

Common Pests

Coffee Berry Borer Leaf Rust Nematodes

Detailed Information

Coffee plants take 3-4 years to produce berries and remain productive for 20-30 years. Arabica grows at higher elevations (900-2000m) with milder temperatures, while Robusta thrives at lower elevations (0-900m) with warmer conditions. Pruning is essential to maintain productivity. Coffee requires a distinct dry period for flowering and uniform rainfall for berry development.

{% elif prediction_text == 'Jute' %}

Jute

Corchorus olitorius/capsularis

Cultivation Details

Soil Type: Alluvial sandy loam
Climate: Warm and humid (24-37°C)
Season: Pre-monsoon (March-April)
Full Sun pH 6.0-7.5 High Rainfall

Care Instructions

Watering: 1500-2500mm rainfall needed
Fertilizer: 40-60 kg N, 20-30 kg P₂O₅/ha
Spacing: 25-30 cm between plants

Common Pests

Stem Rot Jute Hairy Caterpillar Anthracnose

Detailed Information

Jute is a bast fiber crop cultivated mainly for its strong natural fibers. The crop matures in 120-150 days. Two main species are cultivated: Corchorus olitorius (Tossa jute) and C. capsularis (White jute). Jute requires standing water during early growth but needs drainage before harvest. Retting (rotting) process in water is essential to extract fibers.

Kidney Beans

Phaseolus vulgaris

Cultivation Details

Soil Type: Well-drained loamy soil
Climate: Warm (18-24°C ideal)
Season: Spring after last frost
Full Sun pH 6.0-7.0 Frost Sensitive

Care Instructions

Watering: Regular, 1 inch per week
Fertilizer: Low nitrogen, high phosphorus
Spacing: 4-6 inches apart in rows

Rice

Oryza sativa

Cultivation Details

Soil Type: Clay loam with good water retention
Climate: Warm and humid (20-37°C)
Season: Kharif and Rabi seasons
Full Sun pH 5.0-6.5 Water Intensive

Care Instructions

Watering: Continuous flooding (5-10cm depth)
Fertilizer: 100-120 kg N, 40-60 kg P₂O₅/ha

Rice cultivation contributes significantly to methane emissions.
